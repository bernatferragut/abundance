How the whole plan works  ›

The idea

We own nine things across two accounts. Every holding has a floor — the amount we keep no matter what. Those floors add up to about 84% of the money, and that part never needs to be sold.

The TFSA holds the floors. The RRSP holds everything that moves. It isn't "good" investments versus "bad" ones — it's the still part and the moving part. The anchor and the sail.

Why the TFSA is never touched

If a TFSA is judged to be running a trading business, the government can tax everything inside it at full rates. The tax-free protection is lost, the contribution room does not come back, and no new room is created for the money withdrawn to pay the tax. It is one-way and permanent. RRSPs are treated differently, which is why all the activity happens there.

The two settings

Setting X is normal conditions — we own more chips, AI power and bitcoin. Setting Y is trouble — the RRSP drops to three holdings, half of it cash, with gold and commodities for the rest. Switching sells the risky things and buys the safe ones.

The signal

Two things are checked against a 200-day average: the ratio of chips to gold, and chips on their own. Both must be strong for Setting X. Either turning weak means Setting Y. There is a deliberate gap between the two triggers, so the plan does not flip back and forth — it changes only about once or twice a year.

What to expect

Around 6–7% a year in a typical year, roughly a 2-in-3 chance of beating inflation, and a drop of about 20% at some point along the way.

This plan will lose money in some years. That is expected, not a failure. It is built to survive bad years, not to avoid them — and it only works if it is held through them. Selling in a panic is the one reliable way to turn a temporary drop into a permanent loss.

The signal is not crash protection. In a sudden crash it saves almost nothing. It helps in slow, grinding declines. The cash and the gold are the real defence.

Once a year

Check the TFSA is still near 60% of the total. Adjust TFSA holdings only by adding new money — never by selling.
